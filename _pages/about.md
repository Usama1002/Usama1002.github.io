I am the AI Initiative Team Lead at [Braindeck Inc.](https://www.braindeck.net/) in Seoul, where I lead work on speech and voice AI for Korean-language users with dysarthria. This includes a custom automatic speech recognition model built for dysarthric speech, integrated into a real-time avatar communication platform, and an end-to-end pipeline for diagnosis, rehabilitation, and communication assistance. My work at Braindeck also covers multimodal emotion recognition from speech, facial expressions, and biosignals.

I received my Ph.D. in [Electrical Engineering](https://ee.kaist.ac.kr/en/) from the [Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST)](https://www.kaist.ac.kr/en/) in 2026, advised by [Prof. Dong Eui Chang](https://control.kaist.ac.kr/) in the Control Lab. I also hold an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from KAIST and a B.E. in Electrical Engineering from the [National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST)](https://nust.edu.pk/), Pakistan.

My research interests span large language models and multi-agent systems, speech and voice AI, reinforcement learning, signal integrity for high-speed memory systems, and machine learning applications in medicine. My [doctoral thesis](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/s7i3v4weorau1520bexan/main.pdf?rlkey=7m19gjj7heujq4jqck7wuhaic&st=pwke8ykf&dl=0) focused on latent representation learning for anomaly detection and reinforcement-learning-based equalizer optimization in high-speed DRAM systems, carried out in collaboration with [Samsung Electronics Device Solutions (DS)](https://semiconductor.samsung.com/).

My [experience](/experience/), [education](/education/), and [publications and patents](/publications/) each have their own page, and a [PDF version of my CV](/files/Muhammad-Usama-CV.pdf) is also available. You can also find my work on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=OyBykQQAAAAJ&hl=en) and [GitHub](https://github.com/Usama1002).
